Spreading Joy, Inspiring Generosity & Supporting Families

Welcome to the official home of SANTACONKCK FOUNDATION. We are a dedicated 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization uniting compassionate neighbors, community volunteers, and local supporters to deliver seasonal relief, family assistance, and charitable outreach across Kansas City, Kansas, and beyond.

Our Enduring Dedication to Community Generosity and Care

At SANTACONKCK FOUNDATION, we believe that the spirit of giving should uplift communities all year long. Operating from our headquarters at 5630 Edgehill St, Kansas City KS 66106, our organization stands as a beacon of goodwill, organizing community drives, festive charity events, and direct family support programs. We recognize that lasting positive impact happens when neighbors come together with open hearts, pooling resources and volunteer energy to bring comfort, joy, and essential supplies to local households in need.

Delivering Essential Relief & Strengthening Local Bonds

Community generosity and neighborly care are the cornerstones of resilient neighborhoods. SANTACONKCK FOUNDATION actively collaborates with local civic partners, businesses, and regional volunteers to organize toy drives, winter relief initiatives, and family aid distributions.

Whether we are coordinating seasonal charity gatherings, assembling care packages for underserved youth, or supporting local food and clothing banks, our focus remains firmly on ensuring that every family feels valued, supported, and included.
